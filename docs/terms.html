What to expect

  • The Windows app lives in your system tray. Depending on your settings, it can show a larger shrimp in the corner of your screen, launch when you sign in to Windows, and restart its countdown when you're idle, unlock your PC or wake it from sleep. You can change these from its menu and quit it at any time.
  • The browser version only works while its tab is open. It can show notifications and an always-on-top floating window if you turn them on and your browser supports them. Browsers may delay timers or notifications in background tabs, so reminders may not always arrive on time.
  • The home page shrimp is a sped-up demo, not a real reminder.
