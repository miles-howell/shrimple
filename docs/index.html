Shrimple

A tiny shrimp that reminds you to sit up straight.

No sounds. No notifications. Just a shrimp in your tray, slouching the way you are and getting steadily angrier about it.

How it works

Shrimple puts a 16×16 shrimp in its own little patch of sea in your Windows system tray. When it's time to check your posture, the shrimp starts slouching, probably the way you are.

Ignore it and it curls up like a shrimp, gets steadily angrier, and climbs out of the tray to glare at you from the corner of your screen. Sit up, click it, and it calms down.

    Using it

    Hover over the shrimp to see how long until the next check. Left-click it when you've sat up straight. Right-click for the menu:

    ^ENG10:42 AM

    How long until the shrimp checks on you. It says "Paused" while reminders are off, and "Posture check time!" once a check is due. It's just a label, so you can't click it.

    Same as left-clicking the shrimp. Tell it you've sat up straight and the countdown starts over. It's the default item, which is why it's bold.

    Opens a list: 5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 45, 60 or 90 minutes between checks. 30 minutes is the default. Picking one restarts the countdown.

    The shrimp stays calm until you unpause. Handy for meetings, lunch, or a well-earned slouch.

    Once the seaweed catches fire, a bigger shrimp climbs out of the tray and sits in the corner of your screen until you click it. It never steals focus, and it stays put during full-screen apps. On by default.

    Keeps the big shrimp in the corner of your screen all the time, calm or not. Off by default.

    Launches Shrimple when you sign in to Windows, so the shrimp is always on duty.

    Bye, shrimp. Closes Shrimple and takes it out of your tray.

    Hover over a menu item to see what it does.

    It won't nag an empty chair. The countdown restarts on its own after 5 minutes with no keyboard or mouse input, when you unlock your PC, and when it wakes from sleep.

    Get the shrimp

    Grab shrimple.exe from the latest release, run it, and look for the shrimp in your tray. You may need to drag it out of the overflow area (^) so it's always visible.

    Or build it yourself with Rust:

    cargo build --release

    Rather not run an EXE? Try Shrimple in your browser: it floats over your other windows and sends notifications from a tab.
