Shrimple, no EXE

The same shrimp, living in this browser tab instead of your system tray. Pin it to float over your other windows, or let it send notifications when it's time to sit up.

Notifications A quiet notification when a check is due, and again as the shrimp gets angrier. No sounds. Only sent while this tab is out of sight.
Float on top Opens a small always-on-top window with the shrimp in it. Click it once you've sat up straight.

Keep this tab open (it can be in the background). Closing it stops the shrimp, and there's no tray icon, no "start with Windows" and no overlay over full-screen apps. For those, grab the EXE.
